Explore Zanzibar

Beautiful destinations, historical landmarks, and coastal paradises — ready to book directly on WhatsApp.

Stone Town (Mji Mkongwe)

A UNESCO World Heritage Site filled with rich cultural heritage. This historic heart of Zanzibar features a mesmerizing labyrinth of narrow winding alleys, bustling open-air bazaars, and traditional Swahili architecture with intricately carved wooden doors.

Offshore Stone Town, Northwest Image Placeholder

Prison Island (Changuu Island)

Originally built as a prison and quarantine station, this island is now a peaceful sanctuary. It is world-famous for its colony of giant Aldabra tortoises, some over 150 years old. Features shallow coral reefs perfect for snorkeling.

Book via WhatsApp
Dole Area, Central District Image Placeholder

Jambo Spice Farm

A sensory-rich agricultural walking tour through lush rural plantations to see, touch, smell, and taste exotic spices. Spot cloves, vanilla, cinnamon, and nutmeg, followed by a traditional seasoned Swahili lunch.

Book via WhatsApp
Central-East Unguja Island Image Placeholder

Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park

Zanzibar's only national park, spanning 50 square kilometers of groundwater forest. It serves as the primary sanctuary for the rare, endangered Kirk's red colobus monkeys and features an elevated mangrove boardwalk.

Book via WhatsApp
Matemwe, Northeast Coast Image Placeholder

Mnemba Atoll Marine Reserve

The premier snorkeling and diving destination in Zanzibar. Features an expansive coral reef ecosystem teeming with green sea turtles, colorful reef fish, and wild dolphins swimming in crystal-clear waters.

Book via WhatsApp

Nakupenda Sandbank

A spectacular pristine sandbank that completely vanishes during high tide. The tour offers swimming in brilliant turquoise waters accompanied by a freshly prepared beachside seafood barbecue.

Jambiani, South-East Coast Image Placeholder

Kuza Cave

An ancient limestone sinkhole and underground cave system containing a crystal-clear, fresh groundwater pool. Offers cultural connection with traditional drumming, dance, and cooking classes.

Baraka Natural Aquarium

A unique natural tidal pool sanctuary for rescued sea turtles. Visitors have the highly interactive experience of wading, swimming alongside, and hand-feeding fresh seaweed to these gentle creatures.

The Rock Restaurant

Globally famous restaurant built on a coral outcrop positioned out in the ocean. The experience changes dynamically with the tides, serving upscale fresh seafood with panoramic ocean views.

South-East Coast Image Placeholder

Paje Beach

A dynamic world-renowned hotspot for kitesurfing due to flat lagoons and consistent winds. Features a vibrant beach town atmosphere and hosts local seaweed harvesting social enterprises.

Kendwa Beach

Possesses one of the most beautiful coastlines largely unaffected by changing tides, allowing all-day swimming. Celebrated for breathtaking sunsets, luxury resorts, and beach bonfires.
