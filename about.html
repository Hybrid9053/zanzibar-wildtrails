About Bennsayd Tour and Safaris

We live here, and we love showing people around.

Our Story

Bennsayd Tour and Safaris was started by a small group of local guides. We used to see visitors get the same tired tours everywhere they went, so we decided to do it differently. Our guides were born here, so every tour comes with real stories, not a script.

We run tours all over the island. Stone Town's old streets, quiet forests, spice farms, and the beaches up north in Nungwi.

📍 Based in Stone Town, Zanzibar. We run tours island-wide.
