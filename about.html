Our Story

Zanzibar WildTrails was founded by a small group of Tanzanian guides who grew tired of watching visitors experience the country through a disconnected, cookie-cutter lens. We wanted to offer something different: tours led by people who were born on these islands and raised near these plains.

Today, we run custom wildlife safaris across the Northern and Southern circuits of Mainland Tanzania, alongside island excursions across Zanzibar's coastline, reefs, and historic towns.
