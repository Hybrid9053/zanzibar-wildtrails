Our Story

Bennsayd Tour and Safaris was founded by a small group of Tanzanian guides who grew tired of watching visitors experience the country through a disconnected, cookie-cutter lens. We wanted to offer something different: tours led by people who were born on these islands and raised near these plains.

Today, we run island excursions across Zanzibar's coastline, reefs, forests, and historic towns, from Stone Town's alleys to the northern beaches of Nungwi.

📍 Based in Stone Town, Zanzibar, with tours running island-wide.
